1)ASCII decimal codes:
*Uppercase A-65
*Lowercase a-97

2)Why a computer needs an encoding standard: 
*Computers only understand binary numbers (0s and 1s), so a standard encoding system is needed to assign a unique, consistent number to every letter, digit, and symbol so any computer can store and display text accurately.

3)Example of a character not in basic ASCII:
*Indian Rupee symbol(₹) (supported in UTF-8)
